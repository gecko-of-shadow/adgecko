This package includes trimmed material from EasyList and EasyPrivacy in
two forms:
- Network block rules: hosts from their network filter lines, converted to Chrome
  declarativeNetRequest rules in src/rules/general-network.json.
- Hide rules: element-hide selectors in src/rules/cosmetic-rules.json, used
  to hide leftover ad boxes on ordinary websites.
Twitch, YouTube (family), and Kick are hard-excluded from the hide rules and
from the network block targets.
The extension also downloads new copies of these lists in the background.
See src/rules/LICENSES.md for all list sources and their terms.
