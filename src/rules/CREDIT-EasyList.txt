  Troublesome / malware / threat host lists (trimmed; not whole-country blocks)

Where the list material is in this package:

  general-network.json  Network block rules from all lists, EasyList and
                        EasyPrivacy included.
  threat-network.json   Network block rules from the malware and threat lists.
  cosmetic-rules.json   Hide rules from EasyList and EasyPrivacy.

Updates: the zip holds a packed copy of the lists. The extension also
downloads new copies of the lists in the background. The "gecko-refresh"
alarm wakes it every 3 hours. It downloads a list again at most every
12 hours. A fresh install downloads the lists at once. "Update lists" on the
settings page downloads them when you click it.
